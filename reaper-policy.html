Data & Privacy Policy

The plain-English deal on what One's Reaper does with what it hears.

Version 1.0 · Effective August 16, 2026 · Applies only within the Last Call (30+) Discord server.

This is the honest, complete version of what our bot collects, what it does with it, what it will never do, and the controls you have. No lawyer-speak, no buried clauses. If anything here isn't clear, ask a mod. You are always free to opt out.

In one line: The Reaper only remembers or talks about you if you opt in. It keeps light, friendly things to make chats feel personal — never sensitive information, never automatically. It never sells or shares your data, and never uses your words to train AI. You can see everything it knows and delete it any time.

1Who runs this, and where

One's Reaper (internally, "Last Call Patrol") is a custom bot operated by the owner of the Last Call (30+) Discord community. It runs only inside this single, private, invite-only server. It isn't published to any directory, isn't distributed, and isn't monetised — no paid features, no revenue. It can't affect your Discord account anywhere outside this server.

2Consent — nothing social happens without your yes

The Reaper has two kinds of behaviour, treated very differently:

  • Utility — commands like /weather and /tldr. These build no profile of you; they roast a city or recap a channel. Open to all verified members, no opt-in needed.
  • Social & memory — the Reaper chatting with you, remembering things about you, or referring to you. This never happens until you explicitly opt in.

To opt in, run /reaper opt-in, read the summary, and choose Accept. We record the date and policy version you accepted. Withdraw any time with /reaper opt-out. Declining costs you nothing — utility commands still work; you simply won't be remembered or engaged proactively.

4What the Reaper will never do

Never
Store sensitive information automatically. Health, relationships, sex, money, trauma, family conflict, legal, religion, politics — none of it is stored by the bot. At most a human reviews it, and by default it's discarded.
Never
Sell, licence, share, or trade your data — not to advertisers, brokers, or anyone. The bot earns no money and has no such capability.
Never
Advertise or market to you.
Never
Use your messages to train, fine-tune, or build any AI model, including language models. See section 5.
Never
Contact you off Discord. It holds no email, phone, or off-platform identifier and can't obtain one.
Never
Profile you, rank you against others, model your relationships, or make eligibility decisions about employment, housing, insurance, or anything comparable.
Never
Ask for or store passwords or credentials.

5How AI is used

The Reaper uses OpenAI's API to understand messages and write replies — composing your introduction, judging whether an answer is genuine, holding conversation. This is inference: a request goes out, a response comes back. It is not model training.

Content sent through the OpenAI API is not used by OpenAI to train its models, per OpenAI's API data policy. And as a standing rule of this project, no model will ever be trained or fine-tuned on any content from this server, in any circumstance.

6Who else touches the data

  • OpenAI — receives message content solely to compose and assess replies. Not a broker, ad network, or monetisation service.
  • MongoDB Atlas — stores the server's data, only to provide storage.
  • SparkedHost — hosts the bot, holding data only in the course of hosting.

No data goes to anyone else.

7How long data is kept, and how you delete it

  • Interview transcripts are deleted once your introduction is written — the introduction is all that's kept. If you leave or are removed, your records are deleted so a return starts clean.
  • Learned social facts (opt-in only) are kept until you delete them or opt out.
  • /reaper what-do-you-know — see exactly what the Reaper remembers, as a numbered list.
  • /reaper forget <number> — delete one specific item.
  • /reaper opt-out — the Reaper forgets everything and stops engaging you.

8Sensitive topics — the human-in-the-loop rule

Real conversation sometimes drifts into private territory. The Reaper is built so anything flagged sensitive is never written to memory automatically. Such material is routed to a human and, unless there's a clear benign reason to keep it, it's dropped. The default is to forget, not to keep.

9Age

Last Call (30+) is an adults-only, 30-and-over community. The bot also enforces a minimum Discord account age at entry. This is an entry control, not identity verification.

10Security and breach

Data is held in access-controlled infrastructure. If the database or host were ever compromised, the operator will notify affected members and Discord, following a procedure written down in advance so it isn't improvised under pressure.

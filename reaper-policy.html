One's Reaper — Data & Privacy Policy

The plain-English deal on what the Reaper does with what it hears.
Version 1.0 · Effective August 16, 2026 · Applies only within the Last Call (30+) Discord server.

This is the honest, complete version of what our bot — One's Reaper — collects, what it does with it, what it will never do, and the controls you have. No lawyer-speak traps, no buried clauses. If something here isn't clear, ask a mod. You are always free to opt out.

The one-line summary: The Reaper only remembers or talks about you if you opt in. It stores light, friendly things to make chats feel personal. It never stores sensitive/private information automatically, never sells or shares your data, and never uses your words to train AI. You can see everything it knows and delete it any time.

1. Who runs this and where it operates

One's Reaper (internally, "Last Call Patrol") is a custom bot operated by the owner of the Last Call (30+) Discord community. It runs only inside this single, private, invite-only server. It is not published to any bot directory, not distributed, and not monetised — there are no paid features and it earns no money. It has no ability to affect your Discord account anywhere outside this server.

2. Consent — nothing social happens without your yes

The Reaper has two kinds of behaviour, and they are treated very differently:

  • Utility (open to all verified members): commands like /weather and /tldr. These build no profile of you — they roast a city or recap a channel. They do not require opt-in.
  • Social & memory (opt-in only): the Reaper chatting with you, remembering things about you, or referring to you. This never happens until you explicitly opt in.

To opt in, run /reaper opt-in, read the summary, and click Accept. We record the date and policy version you accepted. You can withdraw any time with /reaper opt-out. Declining costs you nothing — utility commands still work, you simply won't be remembered or engaged proactively.

4. What the Reaper will NEVER do

NEVER Store sensitive information automatically. Health, relationships, sex, money, trauma, family conflict, legal matters, religion, politics — if any of that surfaces, it is not stored by the bot. At most a human reviews it and, by default, it is discarded.

NEVER Sell, licence, share, or trade your data — not to advertisers, data brokers, analytics firms, or anyone. The bot earns no money and has no such capability.

NEVER Advertise or market to you.

NEVER Use your messages to train, fine-tune, or build any AI model — including large language models. See section 5.

NEVER Contact you off Discord. The bot holds no email, phone number, or any off-platform identifier and cannot obtain one.

NEVER Profile you, rank you against others, model your relationships, or make eligibility decisions about employment, housing, insurance, or anything comparable.

NEVER Ask for or store passwords or credentials. It cannot, and never will.

5. How AI is used (and why it's not "AI training on you")

The Reaper uses OpenAI's API to understand messages and write replies — for example, composing your introduction, judging whether an answer is genuine, and holding conversation. This is inference: a request is sent, a response comes back. It is not model training.

Content sent through the OpenAI API is not used by OpenAI to train its models, per OpenAI's API data policy. We have committed — as a permanent, standing rule of this project — that no model will ever be trained or fine-tuned on any content from this server, in any circumstance.

6. Who else touches the data (third parties)

  • OpenAI — receives message content solely to compose/assess replies (inference). Not a data broker, ad network, or monetisation service.
  • MongoDB Atlas — stores the server's data. Holds it only to provide storage; makes no other use of it.
  • SparkedHost — hosts the bot. Holds data only in the course of hosting.

No data goes to anyone else. Full stop.

7. How long data is kept, and how you delete it

  • Interview transcripts are deleted once your introduction is written — the introduction is the only thing kept afterward. If you're ever removed or leave, your records are deleted so a return starts clean.
  • Learned social facts (opt-in only) are kept until you delete them or opt out.
  • /reaper what-do-you-know — see exactly what the Reaper remembers about you, as a numbered list.
  • /reaper forget <number> — delete one specific item.
  • /reaper opt-out — the Reaper forgets everything about you and stops engaging you.

8. Sensitive topics — the human-in-the-loop rule

Because real conversation sometimes drifts into private territory, the Reaper is built so that anything flagged sensitive is never written to memory automatically. Such material is routed to a human reviewer and, unless there's a clear, benign reason to keep it, it is dropped. The default is to forget, not to keep.

9. Age

Last Call (30+) is an adults-only, 30-and-over community. The bot additionally enforces a minimum Discord account age at entry. This is an entry control, not identity verification.

10. Security & breach

Data is held in access-controlled infrastructure. In the unlikely event of a compromise of the database or host, the operator will notify affected members and Discord. A written procedure for this exists in advance so it isn't improvised under pressure.

12. Reporting & questions

To report a problem with the bot, contest a decision (like a removal), or request deletion of your data, use the server's designated support channel — a human reviews it and can reverse any automated action. Consent and deletion requests are always honoured.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, its version number is updated and opted-in members may be asked to review and re-accept. The current version and date are shown at the top of this page.
